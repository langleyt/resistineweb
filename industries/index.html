Government

Building resilient cybersecurity for Public Service

Government agencies—from federal offices to local municipalities—should proactively monitor and manage their infrastructures to protect national security and ensure full compliance with rigorous cybersecurity regulations like NIS2 and other legislative mandates. With complex systems, a high volume of sensitive data, and a shortage of dedicated Cybersecurity professionals, these institutions need resilient, automated solutions that enhance team efficiency and simplify compliance.

Take the use case of a local municipality in Germany, where a shortage of resources and rising cyber-threats could result in an overwhelmed cybersecurity team unable to manage critical updates and ensure necessary protection across all systems.

Regular monitoring of sensitive infrastructure and IT systems

Ensuring compliance with national and EU regulations (NIS2)

Limited cybersecurity resources for comprehensive oversight

Securing sensitive citizen and operational data

Resistine solution

The AI Cybersecurity Assistant empowers government institutions to manage their own security autonomously. With powerful monitoring tools and automated incident reporting, government IT teams can easily maintain visibility over their IT infrastructure and ensure regulatory compliance.

Enable in-house teams to monitor and secure infrastructures efficiently

Provide automated compliance reports to national cybersecurity authorities

Offer a user-friendly dashboard for real-time visibility and data export

Resistine AI Cybersecurity Assistant ensures continuous security and simplified compliance for government agencies.

Small/Medium Business

Cost-effective cybersecurity for growing businesses

Small and medium businesses (SMBs) face distinct challenges, often constrained by limited budgets and smaller teams. They require cost-effective cybersecurity solutions that don’t require additional hires or excessive spending, yet effectively protect against cyber-threats and support compliance.

Consider the use case of a fast-growing tech startup that found it difficult to protect sensitive customer data and maintain compliance with regulations due to limited staff and a small cybersecurity budget, often relying on ineffective and fragmented security solutions.

Budget constraints limiting access to enterprise-grade security

A need for simplified, easily manageable solutions

Limited or non-specialised IT personnel

Growing risk of cyber-attacks threatening business continuity

Resistine solution

The company delivers cost-effective, accessible cybersecurity for SMBs, empowering them to meet NIS2 compliance without the need for highly skilled cybersecurity personnel. With its intuitive interface and AI-driven threat detection, the solution simplifies security management, helping businesses stay secure and compliant as regulations evolve. As executive leadership is directly accountable for cybersecurity compliance with NIS2, the timeline to address these challenges becomes more immediate.

Ease compliance with NIS2, providing clear reporting that meets regulatory standards

Reduce workload on existing personnel with automated threat detection and streamlined management

Scale seamlessly alongside business growth, adapting to new challenges

Enterprise-grade protection without the enterprise price.

High Schools & Universities

Safeguarding student data and learning environments

High schools and universities today face mounting cybersecurity challenges as they manage vast amounts of sensitive data, from student and faculty records to research findings. These institutions must safeguard their networks and data against increasingly sophisticated cyber-threats, yet many operate with limited budgets and IT staff. Compliance with cybersecurity regulations, such as GDPR and other local mandates, is essential but adds another layer of complexity. Also, they often seek solutions that balance both robust protection and educational value.

The use case of a mid-sized German university faced with frequent cyber-threats but lacking a dedicated cybersecurity team, relying instead on a small IT department to manage security and compliance across an open-access network used by students, faculty, and researchers highlights an ongoing issue.

Protect sensitive research and student data

Support cybersecurity education and training for students

Manage a high volume of data across complex networks

Limited budget and resources for cybersecurity

Resistine solution

The AI Cybersecurity Assistant provides high schools and universities with robust security at no cost, empowering IT teams to monitor and protect campus infrastructure while fostering cybersecurity education. Universities need only provide their own dedicated hardware, as Resistine covers the software and updates.

Monitor and protect university infrastructure with minimal IT staffing needs

Support educational efforts by explaining cybersecurity topics in clear, accessible language

Free software licensing for universities; only dedicated hardware required

Secure your campus and train tomorrow’s cybersecurity experts with Resistine AI.
